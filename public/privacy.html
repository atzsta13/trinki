Privacy Policy

Last Updated: December 2025

1. Data Collection

Party Penguin (the "App") collects absolutely no personal data. We do not track your location, your device ID, or your usage habits. Everything stays on your device.

2. Local Storage

The App uses your device's local storage solely to save:

  • Your current game progress (so you can close and reopen the app).
  • Player names (so you don't have to re-type them).
  • Custom cards you create.
  • Settings preferences (like dark mode or sound volume).

This data is never sent to any server.

3. Third-Party Services

We do not use any third-party analytics or ad networks. The App is 100% offline-capable.

4. Contact

If you have questions, enjoy the party!
