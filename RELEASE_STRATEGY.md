## 2. 🛡 Compliance & "Alcohol" Handling
**CRITICAL:** To prevent rejection from Apple/Google "LLM bots" regarding alcohol content:

### 🔞 The "17+" Strategy
You **MUST** select the **17+ (Mature)** age rating during submission.
-   **Apple App Store:** "Frequent/Intense Alcohol, Tobacco, or Drug Use or References".
-   **Google Play:** "PEGI 16 / ESRB Mature 17+".

**Why?**
-   Apps rated 4+ or 12+ **CANNOT** reference alcohol.
-   Apps rated 17+ **ARE ALLOWED** to reference alcohol as a core mechanic (e.g., "Picolo", "King's Cup").
-   **Trinki** refers to "Drinking" (Esperanto/Slang), but by creating a 17+ app, you are **fully compliant**.

## 3. Organic Growth: "$0 Budget Marketing"

### A. TikTok & Reels (The "Chaos Loop")
Party games are visual and loud. Leverage that:
-   **Format:** Split screen. 
    -   Top: App screen (e.g., a "5-Second Panic" challenge).
    -   Bottom: Real video of friends screaming/laughing.
-   **Hook:** "This app has no chill" or "How to end a friendship in 5 seconds."
-   **Call to Action:** "Link in bio - 100% Free."

### B. "Campus Ambassador" Guerilla Tactic
-   Find the "social hub" friends at universities.
-   Give them early access/Beta builds.
-   Growth is driven by word-of-mouth once people see the "Premium" look compared to cheap ad-filled clones.
